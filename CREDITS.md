## Illustrations
All images in `images/` are original storybook-style illustrations generated for Kisti (AI image generation, style matched to Daniel Mozafari's *Dutch in 90 Days* book art). They are artistic reconstructions, not photographs; dress, crowns and buildings are historically inspired but not exact.

- persepolis, cyrus, cyrus-tomb, darius, xerxes, naqsh-rostam, ardashir, ardashir-palace, shapur, khosrow, taq-kasra, takht-soleyman, behistun, zoroaster, mascot (young scribe)

## Texts
- Old Persian spellings follow R. G. Kent, *Old Persian* (1953) and R. Schmitt, *Die altpersischen Inschriften der Achaimeniden* (2009). Behistun DB I 1–3.
- Wedge positions for the mashq tool are taken from the Noto Sans Old Persian outlines; the stroke order shown is a teaching convention, not an attested scribal order.
